ABOUT MESA

Mechanical Engineering Student Association • Sanjivani University

Empowering Future Mechanical Innovators

The Mechanical Engineering Student Association (MESA) is the premier student organization associated with the Department of Mechanical Engineering, School of Engineering & Technology at Sanjivani University.

MESA provides a holistic launchpad for students to participate vigorously in technical symposiums, robotics hackathons, CAD/CAM design challenges, cultural festivities, inter-collegiate athletic tournaments, NSS social initiatives, and executive leadership development.

Technical Edge

Workshops on FEA, CFD & Rapid Prototyping

Comprehensive Growth

National competitions & industry exposure

Technical Mastery

Hands-on exposure to advanced CAD modeling, simulation, and modern industrial tooling.

Leadership & Ethics

Student council stewardship cultivating teamwork, project planning, and communication.

Sports & Cultural

Energetic intra-department matches, annual sports leagues, and cultural talent showcases.

Social Impact (NSS)

Community development drives, environmental sustainability, and rural upliftment.

Academic Excellence

Department of Mechanical Engineering

School of Engineering & Technology • Sanjivani University, Kopargaon

Academic Programs

Cutting-edge curriculum in B.Tech, M.Tech, and Doctoral Research integrated with industry 4.0 standards, smart manufacturing, and automation.

Technical Laboratories

State-of-the-art CNC setups, Thermodynamics, Fluid Dynamics, Metallurgy, Mechatronics, and Robotics experimental centers.

Workshops & Certifications

Intensive certified trainings in SolidWorks, ANSYS, CATIA, MATLAB, 3D printing technologies, and electric vehicle powertrain mechanics.

National Competitions

Stellar representation in BAJA SAE, SUPRA Formula Student, Go-Kart championships, and national hackathons with podium accolades.

Student Development

Rigorous placement prep, soft skills enrichment, mock technical interviews, and direct mentorship from distinguished alumni.

Innovation & Design Center

Dedicated incubation space for patent drafting, product prototyping, sustainable clean tech projects, and startup ventures.
